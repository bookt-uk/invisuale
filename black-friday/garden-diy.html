How to shop Black Friday garden & diy deals

  • Stick to one battery platform. A cheap cordless tool from a different brand can cost more once you add its battery and charger.
  • Check whether a deal is 'body only'. Many power tool offers don't include a battery or charger.
  • Plant spring bulbs now. Bulbs planted from September to December flower from late winter into spring.
  • Garden furniture and BBQs are usually cheapest out of season.
