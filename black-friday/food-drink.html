How to shop Black Friday food & drink deals

  • Work out the unit price. Mixed cases and multipacks look cheap until you compare the price per bottle or per 100g.
  • Check best-before dates if you're stocking up on snacks or coffee.
  • Order Christmas drinks early. Delivery slots get busier through December.
  • Use free delivery thresholds. Combining orders to pass the threshold beats paying for shipping.

FAQs

Is Black Friday good for wine deals?Many UK wine merchants run Black Friday promotions, and it's a good time to buy for Christmas. Compare per-bottle prices on mixed cases.
How do I know a multipack is good value?Compare the unit price, per bottle, per 100g or per item, against buying singles. Retailers usually show it on the product page.
