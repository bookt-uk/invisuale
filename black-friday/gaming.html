How to shop Black Friday gaming deals

  • Bundles often beat straight discounts. A console bundled with a game or a second controller can be better value than a price cut, as long as you want what's included.
  • Check the console model and storage. Bundles sometimes use an older revision or a smaller drive, so compare specs as well as price.
  • Games a few months old drop the most. Brand-new releases and pre-orders rarely see big Black Friday cuts.
  • Look at memberships before you renew. Subscriptions such as PlayStation Plus have often been discounted around Black Friday in previous years.

FAQs
